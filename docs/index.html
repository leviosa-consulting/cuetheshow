A PDF needs selectable text — if yours is a scan, run OCR on it or paste the text instead. Cues can be edited afterwards (double-click a cue in the list). Wrap words in *asterisks* for italics, **double** for bold; italics already in a PDF come across automatically.

Running a show in three steps

  1. Music — add your audio files, put them in show order, and set volume, fades, loops and trims per cue (✎). Groups A–D crossfade within themselves and play over each other; stack plays on top. Press ⏎ GO for each cue at show time — that's the whole job.
  2. Subtitles (optional) — running surtitles or captions too? Switch Console mode to Music + subtitles in SETUP, then drop a script PDF or paste text; it becomes subtitle cues (~8 words, sentence-safe). *italic*, **bold**. Music cues can link to lines.
  3. Show time — with subtitles, click Projector view, drag it to the projector, double-click for fullscreen. The keys:
Space / →next subtitle⏎GO — fire music
←previous subtitleSfade music out
Bblank the screenPpause / resume
Ninsert a cueXstop all (twice = instant)

Everything is saved in this browser automatically and restores on reopen — switch or add shows from the name in the top bar. Export show (SETUP, or ⇩ on a show card) makes one file with script, music and audio — drop it on any other machine to move the whole show.
